Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 06, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Plöntukrot application collects, uses, and stores your information when you use the Service.

Interpretation and Definitions

Company ("We", "Us", "Our") refers to the operator of the Plöntukrot application.
Service refers to the Plöntukrot mobile application.
Personal Data means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
You means the individual using the Service.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Personal Data

When you sign in via Google Authentication (Firebase), we receive:

  • Email address
  • First name and/or display name associated with your Google account

This data is necessary to create and manage your account in the application. We do not collect passwords; authentication is handled entirely by Google.

Photos and Content You Upload

To use the Service, you may upload photos of your plants and related information (notes, names, care schedules). This content is stored in your account and displayed within the application.

Important notice regarding photo metadata: Photos taken with your device may contain metadata (EXIF data), including GPS coordinates indicating where the photo was taken. By uploading photos, you acknowledge that such metadata may be present. We do not actively extract or use this location data for any purpose. However, if you wish to avoid disclosing your location, we recommend disabling geotagging in your camera settings before photographing or using the in-app upload feature, which strips GPS metadata from images upon upload.

Usage Data

We automatically collect certain technical data necessary for the application to function and to maintain security, including:

  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Anonymous diagnostic data and crash logs

This data is collected via Firebase (Google) infrastructure.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the Service
  • To identify you within the application (via your account)
  • To store and display the plant photos and information you upload
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve stability

Service Providers

We use third-party services to host data and process authentication:

  • Google Firebase: used for user authentication, database (Firestore), and file storage (Firebase Storage). Your data is stored on Firebase servers. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how Google handles data.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your Personal Data to any third party.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your Personal Data with third parties except:

  • With Firebase/Google as our hosting and authentication provider
  • If required by law or a valid legal request from public authorities

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your Personal Data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Service. If you delete your account, we will delete your Personal Data and uploaded content within a reasonable time, except where we are legally obligated to retain it.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Security

The security of your data is important to us. We rely on Firebase's commercially reasonable security measures. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will delete it promptly.

The Service may contain links to third-party websites (for example, plant care resources). We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or privacy practices of those sites.
